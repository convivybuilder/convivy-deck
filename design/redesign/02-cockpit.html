The row grammar

Five treatments, the same five CockpitView.RowTreatment defines today, on a 14pt glyph column. Only the ink changes: "in progress" takes the crew's hue rather than the system accent.

Ahead
open. Hollow, secondary. Nothing is asserted about it.
In progress
claimed, pr-open, anything past open. Half-filled, in the crew's hue (neutral when the hue is near the summons).
Held
parked. Pause mark, in the summons hue: a human decides when it resumes.
Done
fixed. Filled check, struck, secondary.
Closed without change
wontfix · duplicate · redirected. One mark: "is it still ahead of me?" has one answer.

What changed from today

  • The header names the project, not the word "Cockpit". convivy-deck in mono, the Course's title beside it, and "12s" for how old the reading is with the refresh glyph, so the manual refresh Cmd-R already binds has a visible home.
  • An index strip replaces the off-screen rail, and this reverses a recorded choice. offScreenRail is absent when nothing qualifies, by design, citing the bead's "furniture, not an alert"; it lists only panels below the fold. The index is always present, which is the opposite call, made for two reasons: the counts make it worth reading even when everything fits (the queue's 23 in the summons hue is the interrupt lane's badge), and a rail that appears only sometimes cannot be a place the hand learns. "Here" is the top-most panel intersecting the viewport, read from the same PanelFramePreferenceKey frames the rail reads. A collapsed panel stays in the index, struck; activating it expands and scrolls.
  • Body text 11pt, secondary 10pt, section labels 10pt tracked and semibold. Every secondary string is --ink-2 at 4.5:1. Controls are 24pt (Approve, Decline, Open terminal); chevrons 10pt; the copy and refresh glyphs 12pt.
  • Rows are a grid: glyph, title, meta. The meta column is right-aligned mono: 1/4 progress, the held count with its pause mark, #143 on the queue's pr-open row (GET /items serves pr; QueueItem does not decode it yet), HIGH on a high-severity row in --attn-text. Chevrons live in the meta column so titles wrap under themselves.
  • The expanded epic keeps the spine and the clamp. State line, "Done when:" at two lines with "more", the severity and claim line, then the children on the same row grammar.
  • Proposals are still their own region, tinted with the crew's hue at 15%, Approve and Decline on the row. No live proposal existed on 2026-09-04, so the text is the multiplayer flavour from the bead.
  • The queue sorts severity first. The one high item is second; the pr-open epic stays first because the queue's own merge puts pr-open ahead of open.

The Crew stats epic draws a held child ("1" beside a pause mark) to show the held count in the meta column; on 2026-09-04 that epic's claim had expired (Last held by: co-convivy-deck-1541) and no child was parked. The held mark is the one row state this page could not show from live data.
