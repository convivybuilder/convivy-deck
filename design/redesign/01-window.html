What changed from today

  • One row above the conversation, not three, with every slot kept. The crew's mark and name; then one flexible slot that draws the co's working-on sentence with its age, unless the pane has something more urgent to say, in which case that pre-empts it in the same slot: "Starting…" during a launch, a render error in red, or the unreachable reason; then the vitals. The states page shows all three. When the vitals are stale, "· as of 4m ago" follows them, as VitalsRow draws it; a stale working-on age turns amber and reads "· 47m ago", as WorkingStrip.durationSuffix distinguishes the two today. They are different facts and both survive.
  • The toolbar shows the two mechanisms as two controls. Terminal View is showingTerminal, a main-pane swap, so it is a lone toggle. It keeps the full name — "Terminal" alone reads as a third sibling of Shell and Local, which are column occupants and a different mechanism entirely (the operator, 2026-09-04). Its way back stays "Session View". Cockpit, Shell and Local are one ColumnOccupant, so they are one segmented control; when the occupant is a viewer, editor or item, a fourth labelled segment appears so the control can always show what the column holds. Re-render and Stop session sit behind ⋯, as they sit behind ⋯ in the status bar today. Controls are 28pt.
  • Vitals become tokens with a context meter. The meter is what a glance reads, and it takes the tier: --ink-2 when ok, --warn past the soft limit, --err at hard. rc draws in both states — --warn when off, ordinary meta ink when on — because a badge that appears in only one state cannot be told apart from one that has not loaded (the operator, 2026-09-05); the subscription moves into the model token's tooltip. The PR token carries the review verdict in --attn when it is "changes requested", the one vital that asks the operator for something.
  • The conversation is drawn as session_view.py draws it: New York for the prose at 15.75/1.65, the operator's turns behind a 3px rule, tool calls as collapsed mono rows. The mock adds the bottom anchor and the fade only.
  • The operator's rows are visibly the operator's. Live state and the composer sit on the chrome surface, one hairline from the canvas. Send is the only filled button in the window, in --accent-fill, solved so white on it clears 4.5.
  • The ribbon gains a position, "waypoint 4", derived from the same Course list the cockpit draws. The ribbon has three states by waypointStyle: done, current, everything else. Crew stats is claimed, so the cockpit draws it in progress and the ribbon draws it ahead; that is the ribbon's zoom, not a disagreement, and both come from one list in build.py.
  • The window tints with the selection. The risk on the overview page; the solver implications are named there.
